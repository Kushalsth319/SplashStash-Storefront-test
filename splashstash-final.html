Our story

Less hassle.
More everyday.

SplashStash started with a simple idea: water bottles should be practical, personal, and easy to take anywhere. We’re developing a bottle that pairs hydration with a removable storage space and sleeves that let you change the look without replacing the whole bottle.

We’re building the concept with everyday students, active people, and families in mind.
